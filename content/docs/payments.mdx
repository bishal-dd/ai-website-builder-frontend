# Payments

Payments are part of the publishing process when you want to make your website live with a domain and hosting.

Your payment summary shows the costs related to your selected domain, hosting, and website service.

## What am I paying for?

Your payment can include:

- Domain price
- Hosting price
- Website generation or service fee

The final amount depends on the domain option, hosting option, and service package selected during publishing.

## Domain price

The domain price is the cost of registering or purchasing your website address.

Example:

```txt
yourbusiness.com
```
