# Generating a Website

Sencill AI uses your business information to generate a complete website draft. The better your input, the better the result.

## How to generate a website

1. Sign in to your Sencill AI account.
2. Go to your dashboard.
3. Start a new website.
4. Choose the website type that matches your business.
5. Enter your business details.
6. Add your services, products, or important information.
7. Submit the form.
8. Wait while Sencill AI generates your website.
9. Open the preview once generation is complete.

## Choosing the right website type

Choose the website type that best matches your business.

Examples:

- Choose **Travel Agency** if you offer tours, packages, or travel services.
- Choose **Hotel** if you provide rooms, stays, or accommodation.
- Choose **Restaurant** if you want to show menus, food, location, and contact details.
- Choose **Portfolio** if you want to showcase personal work, projects, or skills.
- Choose **Business Website** if your company provides services or general business information.

Choosing the right type helps Sencill AI create better pages, sections, and content.

## What information should I provide?

You should provide clear details about your business.

Helpful information includes:

- Business name
- Business description
- Services or products
- Target customers
- Location
- Contact details
- Brand style
- Important pages you want
- Special offers or highlights

## Example input

A simple input:

> Create a website for my travel company.

A better input:

> Create a modern website for a Bhutan-based travel agency offering cultural tours, trekking packages, festival tours, and custom itineraries for international travelers. The website should feel premium, warm, and trustworthy.

The second example gives more context, so the generated website will usually be better.

## How long does generation take?

Website generation may take a short time depending on:

- The amount of information provided
- The number of pages being generated
- The current system load
- Internet connection
- AI processing time

Please wait until the generation process finishes. Avoid refreshing the page unless it seems stuck for a long time.

## What happens after generation?

After your website is generated, you can:

- Preview the full website
- Review the design and content
- Edit text directly
- Change images
- Request improvements using the AI chat editor
- Publish the website when ready

## Why does the result look different each time?

AI-generated websites may vary each time because the AI can create different layouts, wording, and design choices based on your input.

This is normal. If the result is not what you expected, you can edit it or request changes.

## What if generation fails?

Website generation may fail if:

- Your internet connection is unstable
- Required information is missing
- The AI service is temporarily busy
- The request takes too long
- A server error occurs

If this happens, try again after a few minutes. If the issue continues, contact support.

## Tips for better results

To get better generated websites:

- Be specific about your business.
- Mention your target customers.
- Include your main services or products.
- Describe the style you want.
- Add important contact or location details.
- Avoid very short descriptions.
