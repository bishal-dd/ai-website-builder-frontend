# Sign In

After creating your account, you can sign in to access your dashboard, view your projects, edit generated websites, and manage publishing requests.

You can sign in using your email and password, or you can continue with Google.

## How to sign in with email

1. Go to the **Sign In** page.
2. Enter the email address you used during sign up.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. After signing in, you will be taken to your dashboard.

![Sign In page showing email, password, and Continue with Google option](/help/sign-in-page.png)

## How to sign in with Google

You can also sign in using your Google account.

1. Go to the **Sign In** page.
2. Click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account linked to your Sencill AI account.
4. Allow access if Google asks for confirmation.
5. After signing in, you will be taken to your dashboard.

Use the same Google account you used when creating your Sencill AI account.

## What you need to sign in

If you sign in with email, you need:

- Your registered email address
- Your account password
- A stable internet connection

If you continue with Google, you need access to the Google account linked to your Sencill AI account.

## I forgot my password

If you forgot your password:

1. Go to the **Sign In** page.
2. Click **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your email for a password reset link.
5. Open the link and create a new password.
6. Return to the Sign In page and sign in again.

If you use Google sign in, you do not need a separate Sencill AI password. Sign in with your Google account instead.

## I cannot sign in

You cannot sign in if:

- Your email address is incorrect.
- Your password is incorrect.
- Your Google account is not linked to your Sencill AI account.
- Your account does not exist yet.
- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.

## Wrong password

If your password is incorrect, try entering it again carefully.

Password fields are case-sensitive, so make sure:

- Caps Lock is not turned on
- There are no extra spaces
- You are using the correct password

If it still does not work, reset your password.

## Google sign in is not working

Google sign in can fail if:

- You choose the wrong Google account.
- The sign in popup is blocked.
- You cancel the Google sign in process.
- Your internet connection is unstable.
- Google authentication is temporarily unavailable.

To fix it, try again and make sure you choose the correct Google account.

## After signing in

After signing in, you can:

- Open your dashboard
- View your generated websites
- Create a new website
- Edit existing websites
- Continue publishing a website
- Manage your account profile
