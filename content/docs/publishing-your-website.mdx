# Publishing Your Website

After generating and editing your website, the next step is publishing it online.

Publishing prepares your website to go live with a domain and hosting setup.

## Before you publish

Before publishing, review your website carefully.

Check that:

- Business name is correct
- Text content is accurate
- Images look good
- Logo is uploaded correctly
- Contact details are correct
- Important pages are available
- Buttons and links make sense
- Mobile layout looks acceptable if preview is available

It is better to review your website before submitting a publishing request.

## How to publish your website

1. Open your generated website preview.
2. Review the website content and design.
3. Click **Publish**.

![Website preview showing the Publish button](/help/publish-button.png)

4. Choose a domain option.

![Publishing modal showing domain options](/help/domain-option.png)

5. Review the domain, hosting, and website cost.

![Payment summary modal showing domain, hosting, and total cost](/help/payment-summary-modal.png)

6. Submit the publishing request.
7. Wait for the request to be reviewed or processed.
8. Once approved and deployed, your website can go live.

## Domain options

During publishing, you can:

- Search for a new domain
- Purchase an available domain
- Use a domain you already own

A domain is the web address people use to visit your website, such as `yourbusiness.com`.

## What happens after clicking Publish?

After you submit a publishing request, your website goes through a review or approval process.

This can include:

- Checking selected domain details
- Reviewing payment information
- Preparing hosting
- Deploying the website
- Connecting the domain to the website

The exact process depends on your selected domain and payment option.

## Why is my website still pending?

Your website can stay pending if:

- Payment is still being reviewed
- Domain setup is not complete
- Admin approval is required
- Hosting setup is still in progress
- Additional information is needed
- There is a temporary deployment delay

If your website stays pending for a long time, contact support.

## Can I edit my website after publishing?

You can edit your website after publishing from your website preview or dashboard.

After making changes, click the **Republish** button to redeploy the updated version of your website.

Once republished, the latest changes will be applied to the live website.

## Can I change my domain after publishing?

You can change your domain after publishing, but it usually requires additional setup.

If you need to change your domain, contact support or check the domain settings if available.

## What if publishing fails?

Publishing can fail because of:

- Domain setup issue
- Hosting setup issue
- Payment issue
- Server error
- Deployment error
- Missing website information

If publishing fails, try again if the option is available. If the issue continues, contact support.

## Tips before going live

Before your website goes live:

- Review every page
- Check spelling and grammar
- Make sure contact details are correct
- Replace placeholder images
- Test buttons and links
- Confirm your domain name
- Review the payment summary
