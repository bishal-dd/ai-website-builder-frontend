# Editing Your Website

After Sencill AI generates your website, you can review the result and make changes before publishing.

You can edit your website directly from the preview page. This allows you to update content, replace images, improve sections, and request changes using the AI chat editor.

## What can I edit?

Depending on the current editor features, you may be able to edit:

- Text content
- Images
- Logo
- Hero background
- Website sections
- Page content
- Some design and layout details

Some advanced design areas may not be directly editable yet, but you can still request changes using the AI chat editor.

## How to edit text

1. Open your generated website preview.
2. Click on the text you want to update.
3. Type your new content.
4. Click outside the text area or save the change.
5. Review the updated content in the preview.

![Preview editor showing editable text being updated](/help/edit-text.png)

Use clear and short text where possible. Long text may affect the layout, especially on smaller screens.

## How to change images

1. Open your website preview.
2. Find the image you want to replace.
3. Click the image change option if available.
4. Upload a new image.
5. Wait for the upload to complete.
6. Review the updated image in the preview.

![Preview editor showing image replacement option](/help/change-image.png)

For better results, use clear, high-quality images that match your business.

## Changing the logo

If your website has a logo area, you may be able to replace it from the preview editor.

![Preview editor showing logo replacement option](/help/change-logo.png)

Recommended logo format:

- PNG
- JPG
- WEBP
- Transparent background if possible
- Clear and readable

Avoid uploading very small or blurry logos.

## Editing the hero section

The hero section is usually the first large section users see on your website.

You may be able to update:

- Hero title
- Hero subtitle
- Call-to-action text
- Background image
- Main visual content

The hero section is important because it creates the first impression of your website.

## Using the AI chat editor

You can use the AI chat editor to request changes in natural language.

The AI helper lets you describe what you want to change, improve, add, or regenerate on your website.

![AI helper showing a prompt box for requesting website changes](/help/ai-helper.png)

Examples:

> Make the homepage more modern and premium.

> Change the hero section to focus on Bhutan cultural tours.

> Add more trust-focused content to the services section.

> Make the design cleaner with more whitespace.

The clearer your request is, the better the result will be.

## Creating new pages

You can also create new pages for your website using the AI chat editor.

This is useful when your generated website needs additional pages that were not included in the first version.

Examples of pages you can request:

- Adventure page
- Signature page
- Custom landing page

To create a new page, describe what page you want and what it should contain.

Example:

> Add a new Services page with a hero section, service cards, pricing information, testimonials, and a contact call-to-action.

Another example:

> Create a new Tours page for my travel agency and include cultural tours, trekking packages, festival tours, and custom itinerary options.

## Tips for creating better pages

When asking the AI to create a new page, include:

- The page name
- The purpose of the page
- Important sections you want
- Content or services to include
- Preferred style or tone
- Any links or call-to-action buttons

A clear request gives the AI more context and usually creates a better page.

## Where will the new page appear?

After the page is created, it may be added to your website preview.

Depending on your website structure, the new page may also need to be linked from the navigation menu, buttons, or cards so visitors can easily find it.

If the page is created but not visible in the navigation, you can ask the AI chat editor to link it.

Example:

> Add the new Services page to the navigation menu.

Or:

> Link the homepage Services section cards to the new Services page.

## Regenerating a page or section

If a page or section does not look right, you may be able to regenerate it.

Use regeneration when:

- The design does not match your brand
- The content feels too generic
- A section is missing important details
- You want a better layout
- You want a different style

Before regenerating, describe what you want changed.

Example:

> Regenerate the tours section with a premium card layout and include cultural tours, trekking tours, and festival tours.

## Tips for better editing

To improve your website:

- Keep headings short and clear.
- Use real business details.
- Replace generic images with your own.
- Make sure contact information is correct.
- Review every page before publishing.
- Check both desktop and mobile views if available.

## Why are some parts not editable?

Some parts of the website may not be editable because they are controlled by the generated layout or system settings.

If you cannot edit something directly, try using the AI chat editor to request the change.

## Changes are not saving

If your changes are not saving:

- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few seconds and try again.
- Refresh the page only after making sure changes are saved.
- Try signing out and signing in again.
- Contact support if the issue continues.
