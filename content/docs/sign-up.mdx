# Sign Up

To use Sencill AI, you need to create an account first. Your account lets you generate websites, save your projects, edit your website, and manage publishing requests.

## How to create an account

1. Go to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Create a password.
5. Submit the form.
6. Verify your email if required.
7. After signing up, you can access your dashboard.

## What information is required?

You may need to provide:

- Your name
- Your email address
- A password

Make sure you use an email address you can access, because you may need it for email verification, password reset, and account notifications.

## Choosing a password

Use a password that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess.

A good password should include:

- At least 8 characters
- A mix of letters and numbers
- Special characters if supported

Avoid using simple passwords like `password123`, your name, or your phone number.

## Email verification

After creating your account, you may be asked to verify your email address.

If email verification is required:

1. Open your email inbox.
2. Look for the verification email from Sencill AI.
3. Click the verification link.
4. Return to Sencill AI and sign in.

## I did not receive the verification email

If you do not see the verification email:

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure the email address you entered is correct.
- Wait a few minutes and check again.
- Try requesting a new verification email if the option is available.

## Why signup may fail

Signup may fail if:

- The email address is already registered.
- The password is too short or invalid.
- Required fields are missing.
- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.

## What happens after signing up?

After your account is created, you can:

- Sign in to your dashboard
- Start generating a website
- View your created projects
- Edit your website
- Publish your website when ready
