# Sign Up

To use Sencill AI, you need to create an account first. Your account lets you generate websites, save your projects, edit your website, and manage publishing requests.

You can create an account using your email and password, or you can continue with Google.

## How to create an account with email

1. Go to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Create a password.
5. Submit the form.
6. After signing up, you can access your dashboard.

![Sign Up page showing name, email, and password fields](/help/sign-up-page.png)

## How to create an account with Google

You can also create an account using your Google account.

1. Go to the **Sign Up** page.
2. Click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account you want to use.
4. Allow access if Google asks for confirmation.
5. After the account is created, you can access your dashboard.

Using Google sign up is faster because you do not need to create a separate password for Sencill AI.

## What information is required?

If you sign up with email, you need to provide:

- Your name
- Your email address
- A password

If you continue with Google, your basic account details are provided by your Google account.

Make sure you use an email address you can access, because you may need it for password reset, account notifications, and support.

## Choosing a password

If you sign up with email, use a password that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess.

A good password should include:

- At least 8 characters
- A mix of letters and numbers
- Special characters if supported

Avoid using simple passwords like `password123`, your name, or your phone number.

If you continue with Google, you do not need to create a separate Sencill AI password.

## Why signup can fail

Signup can fail if:

- The email address is already registered.
- The password is too short or invalid.
- Required fields are missing.
- Google sign up is cancelled before completion.
- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.

## What happens after signing up?

After your account is created, you can:

- Sign in to your dashboard
- Start generating a website
- View your created projects
- Edit your website
- Publish your website when ready
