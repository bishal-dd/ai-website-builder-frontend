# Domains and Hosting

A domain and hosting are required to make your website available online.

Sencill AI helps you choose a domain option and prepare your website for hosting during the publishing process.

## What is a domain?

A domain is the web address people use to visit your website.

Examples:

- `yourbusiness.com`
- `myrestaurant.bt`
- `bhutantours.com`

A good domain should be short, easy to remember, and related to your business name.

## What is hosting?

Hosting is where your website files are stored so people can access your website online.

When your website is hosted, visitors can open your website from a browser using your domain name.

## Domain options

During publishing, you can:

- Search for a new domain
- Purchase an available domain
- Use a domain you already own

Choose the option that best fits your business.

## Searching for a new domain

To search for a new domain:

1. Open your generated website preview.
2. Click **Publish**.
3. Choose the option to search for a domain.
4. Enter the domain name you want.
5. Review the available domain suggestions.
6. Select the domain you want to use.
7. Continue to the payment or publishing request step.

If your first choice is not available, try a shorter name, a different extension, or a name that includes your business type.

## Using your own domain

If you already own a domain, you can choose the option to use your existing domain.

You may need to provide your domain name so it can be connected to your published website.

Example:

```txt
yourbusiness.com
```
