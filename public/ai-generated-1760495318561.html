Our Story

Druk Dine was founded with a simple dream — to bring authentic Bhutanese cuisine to everyone, served with heart and heritage. Located in the heart of Thimphu, our restaurant blends tradition with a modern dining experience. Each recipe is passed down through generations, celebrating the rich culture and taste of Bhutan.
